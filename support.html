Stampday support

Days aren't being counted

In Settings › Stampday › Location, choose Always. Stampday notes the country once a day using low-power location; with "While Using" it only counts when the app is open.

A day shows the wrong country

Open History, tap the stay and change it, or confirm guessed days in You › Check. Days you enter yourself always win over location, photos and imports.

Importing past travel

You › Import from Photos rebuilds trips from the places saved with your photos. You › Import from a folder or files reads another travel app's backup, Google location history (Takeout or the Timeline export from Google Maps), a CSV list of dates and countries, boarding passes, receipts and card statements.

Rules and advice

Each counter shows its legal basis, sources and when it was last checked. Day counts are a guide to the rules, not tax advice: check your situation with a tax adviser.
