Features

Your residence, counted and backed up

A day count is only as good as the proof behind it. Stampday does both: it counts, and it files the evidence.

Counts by itself

Low-power location notes which country you are in each day. No check-ins, no spreadsheets, little battery.

Proof for every day

Boarding passes, receipts, entry stamps and card payments are matched to the days they prove.

Rules with their sources

Each counter follows a country's own rule, how it counts travel days and its window, citing the law it comes from.

Brings in your past

Rebuild earlier years from your photos, Google location history, other travel apps' backups or a simple CSV.

Every border crossing

Flights, layovers, ferries, trains and road crossings, each with its ticket. It flags the crossings that still need one.

An evidence file for your adviser

One PDF per year: days per country, every trip with its proof, your tax numbers on the cover.

Counters

Know how many days you have left

Set up the counters that matter to you, suggested from where you live, your passports and whether you are moving this year.

  • Stay under a limit, or reach a minimum, per calendar or tax year
  • Rolling windows like Schengen's 90 days in any 180
  • "Not resident elsewhere": every other country's own test at once
  • Each counter turns amber as you get close, so you can plan your next trip
Evidence

The proof a tax office asks for

Day counts can be challenged. Stampday shows which days are backed by something a third party issued, and which are not yet.

  • Receipts and border papers found in the photos you already take
  • Boarding passes from Wallet, PDFs or screenshots, with your name checked
  • Card statements matched with receipts of the same day
  • Days without proof listed, so you can fill the gaps in time
Trips

Every crossing, with its ticket

Your stays and journeys in one timeline. Connections are joined into one trip, and an overnight layover asks whether you left the airport.

  • Stays per country with how each day was known
  • Flights, ferries, trains and road crossings
  • Airside transit never counts as a day in the country

FAQ

Questions

Is Stampday tax advice?

No. It counts days the way each country's rule describes and shows the legal source, but residence also depends on things a day count can't see, like your home, family and work. Check your situation with a tax adviser; Stampday gives them clean numbers and evidence.

Which countries and rules does it know?

Rules for 42 countries, including the 183-day tests, Cyprus's 60-day rule, the UK statutory residence test and Schengen 90/180, each with its sources and the date it was last checked. You can also make your own counter.

Does it drain my battery?

No. It uses the iPhone's low-power location changes instead of constant GPS, and only needs the country you are in each day.

What about the years before I installed it?

Import from your photos' places, Google location history, another travel app's backup or a CSV of dates and countries. Boarding passes and receipts you saved are found and filed too.

What if I change iPhone?

Back up to a folder you choose, such as iCloud Drive, and restore on the new iPhone. Everything comes back: days, proofs, profile and settings.

Which languages?

English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Polish, Greek, Russian and Chinese (Simplified). It also reads receipts, tickets and stamps in many more scripts, from Arabic to Japanese.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Stampday is for iPhone first. Write to us if you would use it on Android.
