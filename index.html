Stampday

Count the days you spend in each country, with the proof behind every day.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • Counts days per country automatically, for tax residence and visa rules such as 183 days or Schengen 90/180.
  • Keeps the evidence: boarding passes, receipts, entry stamps and card payments, matched to each day.
  • Rules with their legal sources for 40 countries, kept up to date.
  • Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no tracking.

Available in English, Español, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Polski, Ελληνικά and Русский.
