Stampday privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Stampday counts the days you spend in each country. It is built so that your travel history never leaves your iPhone unless you choose to send it somewhere.

What Stampday collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive your location, your photos, your documents or any other data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Location. With your permission, Stampday uses low-power location to note which country you are in each day. Locations are stored on your iPhone only.
  • Photos. With your permission, Stampday reads the date and place saved with your photos, and reads photos on the device to find receipts and boarding passes. Pictures you keep as proof are copied into the app on your iPhone.
  • Files you import. Backups, location history, statements and documents you pick are read on your iPhone.

Connections Stampday makes

  • Apple place lookup. To turn a position into a country, Stampday uses Apple's geocoding service, which receives a coordinate (not your identity). See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Residence rules. Stampday may download an updated, signed list of residence rules from this website. The request carries no personal data.

Sharing and backups

Data leaves your iPhone only when you do it: when you share an evidence file or export, or save a backup to a folder you choose (for example iCloud Drive or OneDrive). Those services' own policies then apply.

Deleting your data

Delete everything in Stampday › You › Delete all travel data, or delete the app.

Children

Stampday is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
